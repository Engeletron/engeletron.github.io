◢ Engeletron
Brazilian electrical engineering, now in silicon

Engeletron Engenharia Elétrica started as a Brazilian electrical engineering firm and now designs RF integrated circuits. We operate on the fabless model: conception and design are ours; manufacturing is carried out by specialized partners.

◢ We design the silicon

From IC design to fabricated and diced wafer.

Engeletron does not merely use chips — we design the integrated circuit. The EETX433A originates as a die on a silicon wafer, manufactured and diced into thousands of units, packaged in SOIC‑8.

◢ Conception & Design
Brazil — Engeletron

The concept, RF architecture, and design/IP of the EETX433A are ours, created in Brazil.

◢ Fabrication & Wafer
Partners — China

Wafer production and packaging are carried out by partner foundries in China, which bring together the equipment, expertise, and cost structure needed to manufacture at quality scale.

Fabless model, with full transparency: the design and IP of the EETX433A belong to Engeletron; manufacturing is handled by partners with the infrastructure to do it — the same way the world's largest chip companies operate.
