Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the ENGELETRON website, applications, or services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information provided by the user: Name, e-mail, phone, and other data entered voluntarily when using our services or contacting us.
  • Usage information: Data about how you interact with the website or application, such as pages visited, features used, and time of use.
  • Device information: Model, operating system, device identifiers, IP address, and similar technical data, collected automatically to improve the user experience.

2. Use of Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve our services and applications;
  • Personalize the user experience;
  • Contact you for support, technical notifications, updates, and service-related communications;
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Information Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • To comply with legal obligations or requests from competent authorities;
  • With suppliers and partners who assist in providing the services, always observing security and confidentiality standards;
  • With your express consent.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to improve navigation on our website or application, analyze trends, and collect statistical information. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may limit some features.

5. Information Security

We adopt technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. User Rights (LGPD)

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time, as well as limit or revoke consent for the use of the information, as provided for in the applicable legislation (Brazilian General Data Protection Law – LGPD).

7. Data Retention

We will retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless otherwise required by law.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of important changes through the website, the application, or other appropriate means.
